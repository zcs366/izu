# Logo Design Concepts — 與京 & 美叶 (2026-05-10 session)

## Client Profile

A husband-wife team running two intertwined businesses:
- **與京 (Yujing):** Military/defense education base (山林CS场地 + school)
- **美叶 (Meiye):** After-school tutoring + calligraphy (托管/书法)

The husband is a literary scholar (classical Chinese) and editor; the wife is the operations lead.

---

## Core Design Philosophy

**Distilled from client feedback:**

| Principle | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| One unforgettable idea per logo | "过目不忘" (unforgettable) was the explicit brief |
| Philosophy first, visual second | User said "不着急，慢慢来，再想想logo的设计思想" — deep thinking before execution |
| Three reading distances | 10m: recognize brand type → 3m: see symbol → 1m: discover hidden meaning |
| Concise presentation | Users hate long explanations. Max 3 lines per concept. |
| AI gen is for mood boards, not deliverables | SD1.5, Flux, ComfyUI all failed to produce usable logos |

## 與京 — "Phoenix as Air Force Insignia"

**Brand name origin:** 與京 = "莫之与京" (incomparable) from Zuozhuan (《左传·庄公二十二年》).
Original context: phoenix divination — "凤皇于飞，和鸣锵锵".

**Core concept:** A phoenix that reads as a military aircraft badge. The tension between "mythical" and "military" is what makes it memorable.

**Visual execution:**
- Shape: triangular/chevron overall silhouette (air force insignia feel)
- Phoenix body: negative space forms the character "京"
- Wings: swept back like fighter jet wings, geometric (12-15 straight lines max)
- Tail: three sharp tail feathers like jet exhaust flames
- Border: circular or shield-shaped badge frame
- Colors: army green `#4B5320` + gold `#D4AF37`
- Large format: deep green base with gold phoenix
- Small format: embroidery on caps/arm patches, pure geometric lines

**Reading sequence:**
1. 10m: strong triangular badge → "military brand"
2. 3m: phoenix with swept-back wings → "powerful, soaring"
3. 1m: "京" hidden in the body → discovery delight

**Client corrections received:**
- "凤凰太古典化，要反其道行之" → make it modern, not traditional
- "是凤凰不是鸽子" → needs phoenix crest/beak, not generic bird
- Followed "空军徽章线条美学" (air force badge aesthetic)

## 美叶 — "One Brushstroke Ginkgo Leaf"

**Brand name origin:** 美叶 = beautiful leaf. 叶 (leaf) homophone for 叶 (harmony in rhyme). "美叶之家" = beautiful, harmonious home.

**Core concept:** A ginkgo leaf drawn with ONE continuous calligraphy brushstroke. The leaf IS calligraphy; calligraphy IS the leaf. The brand teaches both.

**Visual execution:**
- Leaf: ginkgo (fan-shaped, most recognizable classical leaf in Chinese culture)
- Technique: modeled after 宋人小品 (Song dynasty album leaf painting) — not watercolor, not realistic
- Stroke: begins with "藏锋" (hidden tip, classic 楷书 start), tapers naturally
- Leaf veins: 3-5 fine ink strokes, doubled as book page creases
- Base: warm paper-toned background (仿古宣纸色 `#F5E6CC`)
- Colors: ginkgo in sage green `#5B8C5A` → `#4A6741` for veins
- Text: "美叶之家" in running script at bottom, horizontal
- Small format: solid leaf silhouette only, screen-printable in one color

**Brand pair logic:** 與京 = fire (phoenix, gold, power), 美叶 = wood (leaf, green, growth). Fire feeds wood → base brand empowers tutoring brand. Wood feeds fire → tutoring supplies students to base.

**Client corrections received:**
- "美叶之家全分开了，所有元素要连接在一起" → all elements must be visually connected
- Logo_00003_ had right atmosphere but wrong plant (flower → ginkgo)
- Wanted 古典至美 (classical beauty), not watercolor or linear

## Workflow That Worked

1. Deep design thinking (Phase 1) → user explicitly asked for this
2. Written design spec with hex codes and philosophy (Phase 2) → validated before any visual
3. Excalidraw hand-drawn prototypes (Phase 3) → shareable links, pixel-exact
4. Iteration via text spec update → new Excalidraw → user feedback loop
5. Final: spec goes to professional designer for vectorization

**What did NOT work:**
- ComfyUI + SD1.5 for logo generation (model can't do geometric phoenix)
- Flux FP8 for logo generation (overpaints, loses precision at small size)
- Any AI image gen before philosophy was locked
